Exemptions — Who Does NOT Have to Meet the Work Requirement

If you are exempt, you skip the 80-hour / $580 test entirely and you stay on annual (not 6-month) renewals. Exemptions are verified at application and at every renewal — you must still be in the exempt category at that point.

Optional Short-Term Hardship Exemptions — California Elected All

These are state-optional under federal law. California chose to implement all of them per the DHCS Implementation Plan (Jan 29, 2026). They are temporary — they excuse the work test for the duration of the hardship, not permanently.

Does the user’s profile qualify for any exemption?

Based on the information provided (property manager + Kalshi trader; no mention of dependent children ≤13, disabilities, pregnancy, veteran status, or Medicare), no standing exemption is assumed. The analysis therefore relies on work-requirement pathways (P1–P5) rather than P6. If the user later meets an exempt category (e.g., becomes a caregiver for a disabled relative, is determined medically frail), they would move to annual renewals and no work test — documentation required.

Exemptions are determined by the county at application and each renewal. They are not retroactive — you must meet the exempt status during the month being evaluated.
