California Individual Mandate (Exempt Surcharge) — Exemptions When You Have No Wages

California requires minimum essential coverage (MEC) or a penalty on your state return. Coverage via Medi-Cal or a Marketplace plan satisfies it; several exemptions are income-based, which matters with zero wages.

MND-01 The requirement Since Jan 1, 2020 California residents (and spouses/domestic partners and dependents) must have MEC each month, hold an exemption, or pay the Individual Shared Responsibility penalty with the state return. Medi-Cal and Covered CA plans are MEC — covered all year means simply checking the box on Form 540 (line 92 for 2025). MND-04 Exemption: income below the tax filing threshold A household whose income is below the federal tax filing threshold is exempt from the mandate penalty — claimed on the state return (FTB 3853). With $0 earned income, whether you qualify depends on total gross income (gains, rent, interest) vs the threshold (e.g., $16,100 single for TY2026). MND-05 Exemption: unaffordable coverage Coverage is deemed unaffordable (full-year exemption for the household) if the lowest-priced available plan's required contribution exceeds 7.28% of household income for the 2025 tax year (7.97% for 2024; FTB indexes annually). With investment-only income, a single person at, e.g., $30,000/yr is exempt if the benchmark exceeds ~$2,184/yr — Marketplace premiums typically exceed that at low incomes only when the person is INELIGIBLE for subsidies (above 400% FPL), so this exemption mainly helps high-income-no-subsidy households. >7.28% of income (2025) TY2025 figures ✓ Verified FTB — Personal Health Care Mandate; Covered CA — Penalty Details and Exemptions

MND-06 Exemption: short coverage gap A gap of three consecutive months or less in a year is exempt (once per lifetime) — claimable on the state return. Relevant if you lapse between a 2026 policy ending and a 2027 start you delayed past the enrollment windows. ≤3 consecutive months Current ✓ Verified FTB — Personal Health Care Mandate
MND-07 Two ways to claim exemptions Either claim exemptions retroactively on the state tax return via Form FTB 3853 (based on actual prior-year facts), or prospectively during the year via Covered California, which reviews and issues an Exemption Certificate Number (ECN) to put on FTB 3853. Covered CA processes hardship, religious, affordability and similar exemptions. FTB 3853 now / Covered CA ECN in-year Current ✓ Verified Covered California — Penalty Details and Exemptions

MND-08 Why enrolling usually moots the penalty entirely If you hold Medi-Cal or a Marketplace plan all 12 months (both count as MEC), the mandate is satisfied — no penalty, no exemption filing; just check the full-year coverage box. For a zero-earned-income Californian, obtaining coverage is therefore also the cheapest way to avoid the surcharge. Full-year MEC = box checked, done Current ✓ Verified FTB — 2025 Personal Income Tax Booklet (Line 92 guidance)
