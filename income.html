Kalshi event contracts — tax classification is still flagged

Do not choose a tax treatment because it would produce better Medi-Cal or marketplace eligibility. The current official sources here establish general tax rules and the CFTC status of the exchange, not the federal income-tax character of the individual's particular event-contract trades. The gross payout, deposits, nominal stake and platform withdrawal are not automatically taxable profit. Work from actual records and qualified tax advice; then report the resulting income correctly to the county/exchange. [IRS Publication 550 · CFTC Release 8302-20 · DHCS MAGI chart]

Timing, averaging and eligibility

California's enacted assessment uses the preceding month at application and a month between determinations at redetermination. A prior-six-month average under §14005.69(e)(7) is limited to seasonal workers. Do not infer that variable trading, vacancies or irregular interest automatically qualify. California dates the work rule to January 1, 2027 and six-month eligibility checks to March 1, 2027. [California §14005.69(d)–(e) · DHCS implementation timeline]

For enrollment, the 2026 one-person Medi-Cal chart gives $22,025/year and $1,836/month; the 2026 Covered California dashboard uses $62,600 at 400% FPL. For the 2027 marketplace, the 400% figure is $63,840. All other requirements and household facts still apply. See the full dated correction and flagged questions.
