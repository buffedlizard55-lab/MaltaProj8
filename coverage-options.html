First question

Which program are you in?

The MAGI New Adult Group generally uses up to 138% FPL. Marketplace financial help is conditional on income and other eligibility rules; 2026 and 2027 use different FPL references. The work rule applies only to affected Medicaid adults.

No work requirement outside Medi-Cal

Covered California, Medicare, and community health centers do not impose the community engagement rule.

Do not assume you can fail Medi-Cal and get a subsidy. Failing the work rule makes you deemed Medicaid-eligible and blocks federal premium tax credits.

If you fail the Medicaid work rule

You do not automatically fall into a subsidized marketplace plan. The statute deems a noncompliant person Medicaid-eligible and makes them ineligible for federal premium tax credits. The practical options are: cure within 30 days, reapply for Medicaid when you can show compliance, or buy a plan at full price. This is the single most important reason to keep at least one qualifying month per renewal period.

If your MAGI is above 138% FPL

You are not in the Medi-Cal MAGI New Adult Group, so the community engagement rule does not apply to you. The relevant question becomes marketplace financial help:

  • 100%–400% FPL: Covered California with the federal premium tax credit; the 2026 applicable percentage ranges from 2.10% to 9.96% of household income for the benchmark Silver plan.
  • At or below 165% FPL in 2026 / 200% FPL in 2027: the California state subsidy may further reduce your share.
  • Above 400% FPL: no federal premium tax credit for 2026. You can still buy a plan at full price; the site does not estimate the premium because it varies by age, rating region and plan.
