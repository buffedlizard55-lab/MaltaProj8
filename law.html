What counts toward $580? The statute says "monthly income." The IFC and DHCS interpret this as gross income from any countable source — see Income Types → for line-by-line mapping of each source (rental, interest, capital gains, unemployment, SSDI, retirement, etc.) against official DHCS/CMS lists. The $580 figure floats with the federal minimum wage — if Congress raises the wage, the threshold rises proportionally.

What the Law Does NOT Require

Not earned-income-only

The $580 threshold is monthly income, not earned income. Unearned counts. You can pass with rental + interest + capital gains and zero wages.

Not every month

At renewal, California will require qualifying activity in any month since last renewal (at least one month), not every month in the period.

Not a re-application

Noncompliance does not bar future applications — you can cure and re-apply. But loss of coverage period means uninsured gap.

Not retroactive

Applies only on or after applicable renewal / application date after Jan 1 2027. No retroactive clawback.
