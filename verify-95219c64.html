The user's question, answered and verified

Profile: U.S. citizen, California resident, property manager + full-time Kalshi trader, income only from Kalshi futures, rental income, and savings interest — 0 earned income.

Answer (verified): Under H.R.1 §71119, the community-engagement requirement offers an income alternative: an individual meets it for a month if their monthly income is at least the federal minimum wage ($7.25) × 80 hours = $580. California's DHCS guidance (ACWDL 25-30 and the MEDIL I26-25 member letter) states this income may be "from any source" and is verified against total gross income for the month. Therefore rental income, bank interest, and Kalshi trading gains all count toward the $580 test. A person with $580/month of unearned income satisfies the requirement without any work, volunteering, or school. This is confirmed by official DHCS sources (links in the table below).

Caveat that still applies: the income test only matters if you are actually subject to the rule — i.e., enrolled in Medi-Cal's MAGI New Adult Group (M1), a non-exempt adult 19–64 with MAGI ≤ 138% FPL ($22,025/yr single in 2026). If your income is above 138% FPL you would be on Covered California, which has no work requirement. See Coverage Options.

Minor nuances / items for the user's review (not errors)

None of the items below change the central answer. They are wording/characterization nuances in the published pages, listed here for transparency so the user can review them after the site is live.

IDItemDetail
N-1 Gross vs. net income for the $580 test ACWDL 25-30 p.7 states the test uses an individual's "total gross income for a month." The dashboard summarizes this as "net rental + Kalshi gains + interest." The direction is correct (unearned income counts), but the precise gross-vs-net treatment of Kalshi trading gains is not explicitly spelled out in ACWDL 25-30. Confirm with a CPA / county worker how trading P&L is counted.
N-2 Member-letter start date says "March 2027" The DHCS MEDIL I26-25 member outreach letter says the rules "will not go into effect until March 2027," while ACWDL 25-30 and the federal statute set the effective date at January 1, 2027. The site uses Jan 1, 2027 (the controlling statutory/ACWDL date). Watch for a possible phased member-facing start.
N-3 "Lead plaintiff" phrasing The site says California is "Lead plaintiff in Massachusetts v. Oz." The caption of the case is Commonwealth of Massachusetts et al. v. Oz; Massachusetts is the lead plaintiff and California is among the plaintiff states. Wording is slightly loose, not wrong.
N-4 $1.1B characterization The site says "$1.1B to implement … to minimize procedural coverage loss." The $1.1B GF (2026-27) is the cost of H.R.1 Medi-Cal policy changes overall — including the state's decision not to backfill certain lost federal funding — not a pure "implementation" line item. The figure itself is accurate.
N-5 Kalshi federal tax treatment still unruled As flagged on the site, no IRS Revenue Ruling/Regulation/Notice classifies Kalshi contracts as of Oct 2026. Capital-gains vs. gambling vs. §1256 treatment changes MAGI, loss offset, and the new 90% gambling-loss cap. This remains an open question, not resolved by any official source — consistent with the site's own flag.
