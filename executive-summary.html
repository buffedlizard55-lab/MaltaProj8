Bottom line

Several conditional routes

CMS expressly counts qualifying in-kind property-management work. The income alternative requires $580 of monthly household income under MAGI-based methods. Exemptions, community service, school, and work programs are other possible paths; none is assumed for this reader.

What California is not doing

California is not waiving the rule for people with zero earned income. It is implementing the federal rule and adding outreach, ex parte checks, a 30-day cure period, and a Covered California state subsidy.

The biggest risk is not the work itself. It is missing a renewal notice or failing to cure in 30 days. Keep your address current and open every county letter.
Want the sequence rather than the summary? The playbook sets out the four routes, the income test, the evidence pack, the calendar and the failure path.

Bottom line

  1. Zero earned income is not zero MAGI. Countable net rental income and savings interest can matter. Whether and how Kalshi event-contract results enter this household’s MAGI budget is unresolved; a trading payout is not automatically income.
  2. Gate 1 is the Medi-Cal income limit. For a one-person household in 2026, MAGI must be at or below $22,025/year ($1,836/month). MAGI Medi-Cal has no asset test. The reader’s household size and countable amount remain unknown.
  3. Gate 2 is the community engagement rule. If you are in the affected group, you need one qualifying month per renewal period. CMS expressly counts property-management duties exchanged for free or reduced rent toward 80 hours; owner-management alone needs county verification. Alternatively, enacted California law allows $580 of monthly income determined with household MAGI-based methods.
  4. Covered California has no work requirement. If your MAGI is between 138% and 400% FPL, you may qualify for federal help and, at or below 165% FPL in 2026 / 200% FPL in 2027, a California state subsidy. Above 400% FPL, the federal credit is unavailable for 2026.
  5. Failing the Medicaid work rule blocks marketplace subsidies. You are deemed Medicaid-eligible, so you cannot get federal premium tax credits. The fallback is cure, reapply, or pay full price.

The two gates, in order

G1

Gate 1 — Are you in the Medi-Cal MAGI New Adult Group?

Medi-Cal's MAGI New Adult Group covers adults ages 19–64 who are California residents, not pregnant, not entitled to or enrolled in Medicare, and whose household MAGI is at or below 138% of the federal poverty level. For a one-person household in 2026 that is $22,025/year or $1,836/month. There is no asset test for MAGI Medi-Cal.

If yes: Go to Gate 2. DHCS targets Jan 1, 2027 for the work rule, subject to enacted implementation conditions; screen for exclusions and exemptions.

If no: If household MAGI is above the Medi-Cal adult-expansion limit, review Covered California. Marketplace help requires separate income, other-coverage and tax-filing eligibility; the 2026 single-person 400% FPL reference is $62,600. It is not guaranteed.

Source: DHCS eligibility chart ↗

G2

Gate 2 — Can you show one qualifying month per renewal period?

If you are in the MAGI New Adult Group and not excluded or exempt, you must demonstrate community engagement for at least one month between eligibility determinations (the months need not be consecutive). In that month you need one of: 80 hours of work (including in-kind or unpaid work), 80 hours of community service, 80 hours in a work program, at least half-time education, a combination totaling 80 hours, or monthly household income of at least $580 under MAGI-based financial methods.

If yes: Meeting a valid route satisfies this work-rule condition for the assessed period, provided all other Medi-Cal eligibility and renewal requirements are met.

If no: The county must send a notice and give you 30 calendar days to show compliance or an exemption. Coverage continues during the cure period. If you do not cure, you can be disenrolled and you are NOT eligible for federal premium tax credits while deemed Medicaid-eligible.

Source: DHCS ACWDL 25-30 ↗

The work rule applies only to Medi-Cal's MAGI New Adult Group. Covered California, Medicare, and community health centers do not impose it.

Your strongest routes with zero earned income

Route 1

In-kind property-management work

CMS defines work to include work in exchange for goods or services, and gives the example of a property manager or building superintendent who receives free or reduced rent. The hours spent on those duties count toward 80 per month.

Keep: the lease or rent-credit agreement, a duty list, and a monthly hours log. Ask the county what else it wants.

CMS IFR ↗

Route 2

$580 household MAGI-based income

DHCS's outreach letter says “income from any source” in one month since the last renewal. Enacted AB 2161 §14005.69(e)(6) requires MAGI-based financial methods for the individual’s household budget. Do not count gross rent, deposits or Kalshi payouts automatically.

Keep: rental and expense records, interest statements and full Kalshi transactions. Tax classification and exact income remain unknown.

CA AB 2161 (enacted) ↗ · CMS IFR ↗ · DHCS MEDIL I26-25 ↗

Route 3

Self-employment or fallback activity

DHCS lists self-employment as work. If owner-management is questioned, 80 hours of community service, a work program, half-time school, or a combination also satisfies the rule.

Keep: a contemporaneous log and a named contact for community service.

DHCS ACWDL 25-30 ↗
