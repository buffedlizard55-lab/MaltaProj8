Coverage without earned wages: the conditional answer

A U.S. citizen living in California can seek MAGI Medi-Cal or a Covered California plan without wages. County/marketplace decisions depend on actual household income, age, coverage, tax characterization and other criteria. This summary does not decide eligibility. Long-form source audit (Markdown).
