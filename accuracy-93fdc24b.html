Do not turn possible coverage into a personal eligibility guarantee.

A California resident with no wages may still qualify for Medi-Cal or marketplace coverage, and countable non-wage income can satisfy the future work-rule income alternative. But rent deposits, trading payouts, and the mere existence of interest do not prove this person meets a dollar threshold. Age, household, tax classification and actual monthly/annual countable income are not supplied. The application and county determination decide; see DHCS application guidance and Covered California.

Do not mix poverty-guideline years

The single-person 2026 Medi-Cal chart and 2026 Covered California marketplace use different FPL reference years. The $63,840 figure belongs to 2027 marketplace coverage, not 2026 marketplace coverage. Household-specific amounts and other eligibility conditions are separate.
