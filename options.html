Option A — Medi-Cal (MAGI), if your income is at or below 138% FPL

ItemValueDetailsSource
What it is California's Medicaid program, for adults 19–64 with household income up to 138% FPL

"The MAGI New Adult Group … extends Medicaid eligibility to all adults aged 19 to 64, including those without dependent children and parents with income over 109% but at or below 138% of the federal poverty level." No asset test for this group.

 ACWDL 25-30 — Work and Community Engagement Requirements for New Adult Group DHCS
Why it is the leading option for a zero-earned-income household Your income is not $0 — it is your rental, interest and trading income, and that is what Medi-Cal measures

There is no separate "no income" Medi-Cal program and no earned-income requirement. If your MAGI lands at or under 138% FPL, you qualify regardless of where the money comes from.

 What to include as income (MAGI and countable income types) HealthCare.gov (CMS/HHS)
What it costs Free or low cost for most people

Health, dental and vision. Separate premium rules start July 1, 2027 for some people with certain immigration statuses.

 Medi-Cal (program overview and eligibility) Covered California
The catch From January 1, 2027: 80 hours or $580 per month, and renewals twice a year

This is the rule that changes everything for you. Use the $580 income test (rental, interest, and trading income count) or the 80-hour route, or claim an exemption.

Decision path

  • Step 1 — Find your MAGI. Add up net rental income, interest, trading results and anything else on your tax return. Use the 2026 table on the Income counting page. If you are unsure, run the numbers with your county or a tax professional.
  • Step 2 — If MAGI ≤ 138% FPL ($22,025 for one person): Option A. Apply to Medi-Cal now, year-round, at no cost.
  • Step 3 — If you also have a qualifying disability: check Option B as well. Ask your county whether you qualify for the Aged & Disabled FPL program at 138% FPL, which is not subject to the work rule, and whether your countable assets are under the limit.
  • Step 4 — If MAGI is above 138% FPL and at or below 400% FPL: Option C. Enroll during open enrollment (November 1, 2026 – January 31, 2027) and claim both the federal credit and the California Premium Subsidy if you are at or below 200% FPL.
  • Step 5 — If MAGI is above 400% FPL, or if you lose Medi-Cal for work-rule non-compliance: Option D, unsubsidized coverage.
  • Step 6 — Whatever you choose: from January 1, 2027, if you are on Medi-Cal as a New Adult Group member, make sure at least one month in each six-month renewal window has either $580 of income from any source or 80 hours of qualifying activity — and keep the proof.
  • Step 7 — Keep your contact details current with your county office. DHCS's letter warns that Medi-Cal ends if you miss renewals, and that losing it this way also ends your marketplace financial help.
