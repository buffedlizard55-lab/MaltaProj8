California’s statute uses the same MAGI-based household test.S2 · Federal rule · official PDF

W12Verification and notice rightsOfficial ruleThe state must use reliable available information before asking for more. A noncompliance notice gives 30 calendar days. In California the 30 days run from the date the notice is received (deemed 5 days after its date), and coverage continues meanwhile. Other bases of eligibility and a fair hearing must be offered before denial.Correction replaces §§435.557–435.558. From January 1, 2028 documentation is required when reasonably available, but no denial may rest solely on documentation that does not exist. Do not ignore a request because a data match might exist; no zero-paperwork guarantee.S1 · CMS rule overview
