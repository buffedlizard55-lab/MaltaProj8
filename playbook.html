Read this first

The rule tests hours or income — never a paycheck

“Work” includes in-kind work and unpaid work. The income alternative counts unearned MAGI. A W-2 is not required for either.

42 CFR 435.552(b), (f)(2)

Do not guess the month. Hours and income are tested month by month. Keep a log that shows which month each hour and each dollar falls in.

Three questions, in order

Question 1

Are you in the group the rule covers?

The rule applies to non-pregnant adults 19–64 in the MAGI New Adult Group (or a listed 1115 demonstration), not entitled to or enrolled in Medicare Part A or enrolled in Part B. The MAGI group has no asset test, so savings and property are not tested as assets for MAGI coverage; income and other eligibility conditions still apply.

42 CFR 435.603 ↗ · CMS IFR ↗

Question 2

Are you excluded or excepted anyway?

The county must screen for exclusions and exceptions before asking you to prove hours: Medicare, a parent or caretaker of a child 13 or younger or of a disabled person, medically frail, recent inmate, and the state-option hardship categories.

DHCS ACWDL 25-30 ↗ · CMS IFR ↗

Question 3

Which route can you prove?

Pick the route you can document with real records for one month. For a property manager, the two realistic routes are documented management hours and net rental income. Neither is assumed for you here.

CMS IFR ↗ · CA AB 2161 (enacted) ↗

Answer question 1 before spending time on the rest. If your household MAGI is above 138% FPL, the work rule does not apply to you at all.

The Kalshi trap. If nonprofessional gambling treatment applies, winnings go into AGI but losses are an itemized deduction, subject from 2026 to the enacted 90%-of-losses rule, capped at gains, and claimed below the AGI line. A losing year could therefore still produce MAGI. Capital or business treatment has different netting and deduction limitations. The character is unresolved — see FL-02 and FL-04 — and a CPA should decide it before you rely on a low-income month.

MAGI starts from adjusted gross income and adds back tax-exempt interest. Net, not gross, is the test for rent. Household income means the income of everyone counted in your MAGI household, so a spouse's income matters.

If something goes wrong

Step 1

Read the notice and diarise the date

Receipt of a Notice of Non-Compliance starts a 30-calendar-day period under AB2161 to show that you were compliant in the month the county reviewed, or that an exclusion or exception applies. Coverage continues during the cure period.

DHCS ACWDL 25-30 ↗ · CMS IFR ↗

Step 2

Answer with records, not explanations

Send the log for the named month, the rent-credit agreement, the Schedule E, or the exemption evidence. Ask the county to confirm in writing which month and which route it is testing.

IRS recordkeeping ↗

Step 3

Ask for a hearing if the decision stands

Federal regulation requires the state to grant a hearing when you believe an action was erroneous, including a denial or termination. The notice states the deadline and how to request one.

42 CFR 431.220 ↗

Step 4

Do not assume a marketplace subsidy

A person who fails the requirement is still treated as Medicaid-eligible for premium tax credit purposes, so a marketplace subsidy is not the automatic fallback. Reapply and ask for an exemption screening.

CRS R48633 ↗

These are procedural steps from the regulation and California guidance, not legal advice. A county eligibility worker or a legal-aid clinic can review a specific notice.

What this page does not do
