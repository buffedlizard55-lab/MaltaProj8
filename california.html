What California Is Doing for Those with 0 Earned Income

California must implement the federal rule on Jan 1, 2027 but is using every available administrative lever to minimize coverage loss — especially for people whose income is passive (rental, investment, trading). The state cannot waive the requirement (no §1115 waiver allowed), but it controls how verification works.

California’s Mitigation — 7 Measures

1. Unearned counted

Rental net (Sch E), interest, capital gains/dividends all count toward $580. No earned-income gate. (MAGI chart →)

2. 6-month averaging

Kalshi’s irregular income averaged over prior 6 months — a zero month does not fail you. (ACWDL 25-30 →)

3. All hardship exemptions adopted

Disaster county + high-unemployment county auto-exempt by residence; inpatient/SNF + medical travel by request. (Implementation Plan →)

4. Ex parte first

State checks EDD/FTB/UI/TANF/SNAP/Medicare data before asking you. Only ask if data missing. (ACWDL 25-30 →)

5. Lawsuit filed

Lead plaintiff in Massachusetts et al. v. Oz (June 2026) — could narrow/enjoin the IFR; outcome pending. (Tracking page →)

6. Outreach 3 months early

DHCS MEDIL I26-25 outreach letters mailed ≥3 months before first look-back, with local opportunity lists. (MEDIL I26-25 →)

7. $1.3B reflected in the enacted budget

The enacted 2026-27 Budget Act reflects about $1.3B General Fund in H.R.1-related Medi-Cal costs, plus $196.8M General Fund for county eligibility workload. The figure often quoted as “$1.1B” is not a work-rule fund; see the correction on Flagged for Review. (DHCS Budget Act highlights →)
