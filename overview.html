California health coverage with no earned income

A research brief for a California permanent resident (or citizen) who works as a property manager and full-time Kalshi trader, has no wages, and has income from rental property, savings interest and trading. Every value below is traced to a federal, state or Covered California source and links to it.

The bottom line

  • You do not need earned income to qualify for Medi-Cal — and you do not need earned income to satisfy the 2027 work rule.
  • Your income is not $0. Net rent, savings interest and trading results are all counted as MAGI income, both for the 138% FPL Medi-Cal limit and for the $580 work-rule test.
  • The simplest path is Medi-Cal if your MAGI is at or below $22,025 for 2026 (one person). You can apply year-round and it costs little or nothing.
  • If you are on Medi-Cal in 2027, make sure at least one month in every six-month window has either $580 of income from any source or 80 hours of work, volunteering, community service, job training or school.
  • If you have a qualifying disability, the Aged & Disabled Medi-Cal program is not subject to the work rule at all — but your assets are counted.

The $580 test does not require earned income "an income from any source of at least $580 (before taxes)"

The member notice DHCS mails to Medi-Cal members says an income "from any source" of at least $580 in any month since your last renewal satisfies the rule. County policy guidance describes it as total gross income, and notes that for fluctuating income (dividends, retirement income, and similar) you can average the prior 6 months. California law defines the test using MAGI-based financial methods.

Who the rule covers Adults 19–64 in the MAGI New Adult Group

California applies the rule to the MAGI New Adult Group (aid code M1) — adults 19 to 64 with income up to 138% FPL — and to state-funded Medi-Cal adults who meet the same criteria. It applies regardless of immigration status.

Members covered under other Medi-Cal groups such as Non-MAGI programs, the Mandatory Children's Group or the Pregnancy Group are excluded from work and community engagement requirements.

What to do now

  • Estimate your MAGI household income for 2026 (not just your paychecks): net rental income, savings interest, and any net trading gains count. See Income counting. If it is $22,025 or less for a one-person household, you are inside the Medi-Cal limit ($1,836/month).
  • Apply to Medi-Cal (or Covered California) right now. Medi-Cal can be applied for at any time of year — not just during open enrollment. One application screens you for both programs. See Apply.
  • Count your monthly income against the $580 test. You do not need earned income. The rule accepts "an income from any source of at least $580 (before taxes) in any month" — rental income, interest and trading income all count. See Work rules.
  • Line up a fallback of 80 hours. If a month falls under $580, volunteering, community service, a work program, or half-time school in that month satisfies the rule. School can be combined with the other hours.
  • Check for an exemption. A qualifying disability, caregiving for a child 13 or younger or a disabled person, pregnancy, CalFresh or CalWORKs work-requirement compliance, veteran with a total disability, tribal membership, and high-unemployment-county residence can excuse you entirely.
