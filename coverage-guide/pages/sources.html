COVERAGE WITHOUT WAGES

Sources & method

Review the primary evidence, exact locators, verification scope, and limitations.

Scope: Medi-Cal adult-expansion work/community engagement rules and coverage alternatives relevant to the stated income sources. This is not an exhaustive tax opinion, a list of all insurers, or a guarantee of future eligibility. The repository initially contained only a one-line README; there was no pre-existing healthcare dataset to audit. Re-verification on October 19, 2026 (arena/75b1956e) re-fetched every source below and re-read each row against it. It added the enacted California statute (AB 2161), 42 CFR 435.552, the federal seasonal-worker definition and Covered California’s 2027 chart.

How this was checked

  1. Read official DHCS, CMS/Medicaid.gov, HealthCare.gov, Covered California and IRS materials via retrieved page text; use targeted official-document search evidence where identified below.
  2. Map every finding to an official source, with a locator and evidence note. “Official rule” means the general statement is supported, not that a person has been approved. “Conditional” identifies analysis dependent on facts not supplied.
  3. Leave unconfirmed values blank on Flagged for Review. A source linked to a flagged row supports the context, not the unresolved answer. No Kalshi-specific conclusion is inferred from general securities guidance.
  4. Check local navigation, assets, source-domain allowlist, unique row IDs and evidence completeness automatically. External pages were retrieved or located in official search results on the review date, not continuously monitored. Automated external HTTP link checks are restricted by sandbox egress; no blanket external-link guarantee is made.
  5. Use the current federal rule and correction with newer California guidance. Do not silently harmonize conflicting official dates, income wording, or rounded thresholds.

S7HealthCare.gov incomeSource reviewedCount income & household size: What to include as incomeFull page re-read 2026-10-19: MAGI; What income types to count; irregular income; Report income changes.S7 · HealthCare.gov income
Evidence note

MAGI is AGI plus untaxed foreign income, non-taxable Social Security and tax-exempt interest. Count capital gains, interest and dividends including tax-exempt interest, "net rental and royalty income" and self-employment income "after expenses". Marketplace savings use "expected household income for the year you want coverage".

S15HRSA sliding-fee careSource reviewedHealth Center Compliance Manual, Chapter 9Page re-read 2026-10-19: Requirements; Demonstrating Compliance.S15 · HRSA sliding-fee care
