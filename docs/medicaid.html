Medi-Cal (California Medicaid)

Free comprehensive coverage for Californians with MAGI up to 138% FPL. The most important fact for you: eligibility is based on total countable income (tax rules), there is NO asset/savings test, and no work requirement is in effect until January 1, 2027.
