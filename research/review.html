2027 transition: important details remain unsettled. Read the Flagged for Review page before relying on a start date or on personal trading hours.

Three material source conflicts

Timing: the DHCS public page and MEDIL body say January 1, 2027 for work rules; the September outreach enclosure says March 2027; the enacted state statute (AB 2161) says “no sooner than January 1, 2027” and only after DHCS confirms its systems are ready. Income wording: the public page describes job earnings, while CMS and AB 2161 use MAGI-based household income and the enclosure says “any source.” Averaging: the enclosure lets people “whose hours change frequently” average income over six months, but AB 2161 and 42 CFR 435.552(g) limit averaging to seasonal workers. All versions are linked below. Do not assume that a generic page overrides an individual county notice.
