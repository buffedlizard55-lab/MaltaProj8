Exclusions & exceptions

Check these before choosing an activity route. None is assumed to apply to you.

Federal terminology distinguishes specified excluded individuals, mandatory exceptions (deemed compliance), optional short-term hardships, and state implementation exemptions. Public California materials often use “excused” or “exempt” more generally. This list summarizes the categories relevant to screening, not automatic approvals.
