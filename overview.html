A research brief for a California permanent resident (or citizen) who works as a property manager and full-time Kalshi trader, has no wages, and has income from rental property, savings interest and trading. Bottom line — conditional, source linked

  • No wages-only gate: MAGI Medi-Cal and Covered California look to household income and other eligibility criteria, not a W-2. DHCS help.
  • Work-rule income route: for covered adults from January 2027, California §14005.69(e)(6) uses at least $580 of monthly MAGI-based household income; a qualifying amount for this person has not been shown. Enacted statute.
  • Seasonal-worker-only averaging: §14005.69(e)(7) does not extend a six-month average to every investor whose results fluctuate. Enacted statute.
  • Separate plan years: 2026 Medi-Cal one-person reference $22,025/year ($1,836/month); 2026 marketplace 400% FPL $62,600; 2027 marketplace 400% FPL $63,840. DHCS 2026 · Covered CA 2026 · Covered CA 2027.

Who the rule covers Adults 19–64 in the MAGI New Adult Group

California applies the rule to the MAGI New Adult Group (aid code M1) — adults 19 to 64 with income up to 138% FPL — and to state-funded Medi-Cal adults who meet the same criteria. It applies regardless of immigration status.

 ACWDL 25-30 — Work and Community Engagement Requirements for New Adult Group DHCS
Who the rule does not cover Non-MAGI groups, children's group, pregnancy group, restricted-scope (emergency) Medi-Cal

Members covered under other Medi-Cal groups such as Non-MAGI programs, the Mandatory Children's Group or the Pregnancy Group are excluded from work and community engagement requirements.

What to do now

  • Gather actual household income records — taxable rental results, savings interest and full event-contract trading records. The person's Kalshi tax character is not established here. See income sources and flags.
