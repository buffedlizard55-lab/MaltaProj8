Verification standard

Each finding has a stable row ID, an official source, a locator and a review date. “Source-supported” means the stated general rule was checked, not that personal eligibility is established. Recommendations are labeled. Unconfirmed values are blank; absence of a verified answer is not proof that no answer exists.
