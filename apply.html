How to answer the income questions

This is where most applications for someone in your situation go wrong. Report your income truthfully and completely, including the non-wage income — and expect the county to see your tax return.

ItemValueDetailsSource
Report your real income, not just your paychecks Net rental income, interest, and your trading results all go in the income section

The application asks for expected household income for the year of coverage. HealthCare.gov lists capital gains, investment income, rental and royalty income, and self-employment income among the types to count. Leaving these out risks a redetermination later.

 What to include as income (MAGI and countable income types) HealthCare.gov (CMS/HHS)
"$0 earned income" is a fine answer to the right question If you have no wages, say you have no wages — and fill in the other income boxes

The rule is not a wage test. The $580 test accepts "an income from any source."

What to gather before you apply

ItemValueDetailsSource
Identity and immigration documents Photo ID; green card if you have one; Social Security number if you have one

Covered California: "List everyone in your household on the application, regardless of immigration status or whether they are applying for insurance." Applying will not affect your immigration status.

 Information for Immigrants Covered California
California residency Proof that you live in California

A standard Medi-Cal eligibility requirement; the DHCS screening tool asks how many people live in your home.

 Qualify for Medi-Cal (screening tool and eligibility chart) DHCS
Income records Lease agreements and receipts for rental income; a 1099 or your own ledger; bank statements showing interest; your trading records and realized gains/losses

Net rental income, interest and trading results are all MAGI income. Keep your own records; the county may ask for proof.

 What to include as income (MAGI and countable income types) HealthCare.gov (CMS/HHS)
If you are claiming an exemption Evidence for it

For example a disability determination, proof of a child 13 or younger in your care, CalFresh/CalWORKs award letters, veteran disability rating, tribal membership, or jail-release records. "Exemption determinations … apply retroactively to the date of application" when you provide self-attestation or verification.

Watch for the renewal notice Renew on time, every six months once 2027 starts

"If your Medi-Cal ends, you have three months to sign up again" (for the immigration-status freeze). Missing a renewal is the most common way to lose coverage.

 Medi-Cal Changes (2026–2027 change calendar) DHCS
Keep evidence of your qualifying hours or income Month by month, in advance

One qualifying month in each six-month window is enough, and the months do not have to be consecutive. Keeping the evidence makes the county's job easy and protects you at redetermination.
