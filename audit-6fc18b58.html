$0 earned income does not automatically mean you fail the work rule.

California’s official member letter provides an income route as well as activity routes. It does not require every person to obtain wages. But your income amounts, age, household size, Medi-Cal category and contract-specific tax treatment are unknown. No personal eligibility result is inferred.

“Have an income from any source of at least $580 (before taxes) in any month since your last renewal.”
DHCS MEDIL I 26-25, enclosed member letter, “Ways You Can Meet This Rule”
Read the qualification: This quotation describes the renewal route. It is not a promise that all deposits, trading turnover, gross rents, or unrealized gains count. The county decides the accepted amount and documentation. Initial-application look-back rules differ. The older ACWDL explicitly calls its guidance preliminary. See the conflicting timing and income wording below.
