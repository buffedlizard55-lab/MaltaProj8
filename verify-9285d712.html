F — Executive Summary (in one paragraph, linked to the full page)

For you: a U.S. citizen, permanent California resident, full-time property manager + Kalshi trader with income only from Kalshi futures contracts, rental income, and savings-account interest (no W-2, no earned income as usually understood): you do not need earned income to obtain or keep healthcare in California. The Medicaid "work requirement" (H.R.1 §71119, CMS-2454-IFC) is satisfied by the $580/month income test using income from any source — your existing net rental (Sch E), Kalshi gains (reported on Sch D / Form 8949), and bank interest (1099-INT) all count, with a 6-month average protecting lumpy trading months. DHCS ACWDL 25-30 and MEDIL I26-25 say this explicitly. If your MAGI is ≤ $22,025/yr (138% FPL single) you are on Medi-Cal (M1, free) with 6-month renewals from Jan 1 2027; if higher (up to $63,840) you are on Covered California with APTC (no work requirement at all); neither program has an earned-income gate. California auto-applies hardship exemptions, uses ex parte data first, gives you 30 days to cure by mail, and is litigating the IFC — but you should still expect to document $580 in at least one month per renewal window or 80 hours of work/volunteering/work-program/education as fallback. Watch county mail. Keep FIFO P&L workpapers — Kalshi does not report trading proceeds on any 1099 — and get a CPA ruling on whether the trades are capital or gambling (the 90% loss-cap phantom-income risk from §70114 after 2025 sits on the gambling side). Full analysis and exact statutory language: Dashboard · The Law · Executive Summary (Markdown).

Not legal/tax/benefits advice. Eligibility is determined by your county and by Covered California on your actual 2026 MAGI and household facts. Law and sub-regulatory guidance may change — verify at the one-click official links above and on Flagged for Review.
