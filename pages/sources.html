COVERAGE WITHOUT WAGES

Sources & method

Review the primary evidence, exact locators, verification scope, and limitations.

Scope: Medi-Cal adult-expansion work/community engagement rules and coverage alternatives relevant to the stated income sources. This is not an exhaustive tax opinion, a list of all insurers, or a guarantee of future eligibility. The repository initially contained only a one-line README; there was no pre-existing healthcare dataset to audit.

How this was checked

  1. Read official DHCS, CMS/Medicaid.gov, HealthCare.gov, Covered California and IRS materials via retrieved page text; use targeted official-document search evidence where identified below.
  2. Map every finding to an official source, with a locator and evidence note. “Official rule” means the general statement is supported, not that a person has been approved. “Conditional” identifies analysis dependent on facts not supplied.
  3. Leave unconfirmed values blank on Flagged for Review. A source linked to a flagged row supports the context, not the unresolved answer. No Kalshi-specific conclusion is inferred from general securities guidance.
  4. Check local navigation, assets, source-domain allowlist, unique row IDs and evidence completeness automatically. External pages were retrieved or located in official search results on the review date, not continuously monitored. Automated external HTTP link checks are restricted by sandbox egress; no blanket external-link guarantee is made.
  5. Use the current federal rule and correction with newer California guidance. Do not silently harmonize conflicting official dates, income wording, or rounded thresholds.

MAGI starts with AGI plus untaxed foreign income, non-taxable Social Security and tax-exempt interest. Include capital gains, interest/dividends, net rental income and self-employment after expenses. Marketplace estimates expected annual household income.

S15HRSA sliding-fee careSource reviewedHealth Center Compliance Manual, Chapter 9Official search excerpts: Requirements; sliding fee discounts.S15 · HRSA sliding-fee care
Evidence note

No patient may be denied service due to inability to pay. Full discount at/below 100% FPG, except possible nominal charge; partial discounts above 100% through 200% FPG. This is care access, not comprehensive insurance.
