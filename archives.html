Why an archive?

The earlier pages are useful detail, but they were written at different times and some contained errors. The core pages correct the highest-impact ones; the archive lets you inspect the original reasoning without treating every row as newly certified.

Status policy

  • Verified — the value matches an official source linked on the row.
  • Verified — nuance — the value is official, but a related definition or date is unresolved and flagged.
  • Flagged — official sources conflict, or the legal question is unsettled.
  • Unverified — blank — no official value was located; the cell is blank.
  • Preserved — earlier work retained for reference; not re-certified by this session.

Source policy: every table row links to an official source (congress.gov, federalregister.gov, cms.gov, medicaid.gov, dhcs.ca.gov, aspe.hhs.gov, irs.gov, coveredca.com, cftc.gov, ca.gov).
