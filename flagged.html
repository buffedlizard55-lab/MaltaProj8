A flag is not a finding of ineligibility

It marks a place where official sources conflict, a value could not be confirmed, or the law is unsettled. The affected cell is left blank rather than estimated.

How to use this page

Take the flag ID to the county eligibility worker, a CPA, or a Covered California counselor. Do not treat a blank as a zero.

Nothing on this page was inferred. Where a number could not be confirmed from an official source, it is absent.

What a flag means

  • Open — two official sources conflict, or the legal question is not settled. Both readings are shown and the reader is told which decision-maker resolves it.
  • Unverified — blank — no official source states the value. The cell is empty by design; an empty cell is never a zero.
  • Closed — an earlier value on this site was wrong. The correction, the old value, and the official source are recorded in the row.

Flags do not block publication. They are published here, labelled, as required. Full source register: Findings → Sources.
