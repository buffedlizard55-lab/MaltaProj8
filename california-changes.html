All of these come from H.R. 1 (Public Law 119-21) and related 2025–26 state budget and trailer-bill action.

January 1, 2026 — adult immigrant enrollment freeze Adults 19+ without a qualifying immigration status can no longer newly enroll in full-scope Medi-Cal

Existing enrollees keep coverage if they renew on time. Children, pregnant people, and former foster youth under 26 are unaffected. Source: DHCS Medi-Cal Changes.

January 1, 2026 — asset limits reinstated for non-MAGI programs $130,000 for one person, +$65,000 per additional person (up to 10)

Applies to people 65+, people with a disability, nursing home residents, and families over the MAGI income limit. MAGI-based Medi-Cal (adults 19–64) still has no asset test. This was restored by AB 116 (Chapter 21, Statutes of 2025) and implemented by DHCS. Source: DHCS asset-limit FAQ.

October 1 – December 31, 2026 — work-rule notice in every application packet Counties must include the DHCS outreach letter with application materials

DHCS began mailing outreach notices to New Adult Group members in late August 2026. The notice explains the work rule, the six-month renewal rule, the exemptions, and the consequences of non-compliance.

January 1, 2027 — work and community engagement requirements begin 80 hours/month, or $580 monthly income, or an exemption or short-term hardship

Applies to the MAGI New Adult Group in California, regardless of immigration status. Applications dated on or before December 31, 2026 are not assessed for the work rule until the member's next renewal in 2027.

 ACWDL 25-30 — Work and Community Engagement Requirements for New Adult Group DHCS
January 2027 — six-month renewals begin Redetermination every 6 months instead of 12, for the MAGI New Adult Group

Section 71107 of H.R. 1. Members are moved from an annual to a six-month cycle at the same time the work rule starts. Tribal members, pregnant/postpartum members, and children are not subject.

 ACWDL 25-31 — Six-Month Renewals for New Adult Group DHCS
January 1, 2027 — Fee-for-Service transition Undocumented people, some green card holders in the five-year bar, and PRUCOL residents move to FFS

This changes how you get care, not whether you keep Medi-Cal. Managed-care-only services (Enhanced Care Management, Community Supports) do not continue in FFS.

January 1, 2027 — retroactive Medi-Cal coverage shortened 1 month for the MAGI New Adult Group; 2 months for all other groups

Previously up to 3 months for everyone. Applies to applications with an application date of January 1, 2027 or later. To get retroactive coverage you must have met the work rule or qualified for an exemption in the retroactive month.

What California is doing to soften the federal changes

ItemValueDetailsSource
State-funded full-scope coverage for immigrants California kept covering immigrants regardless of status, and is now phasing that back

The state-funded coverage that LPRs inside the five-year bar receive is the reason the 2026 freeze did not leave a coverage gap in California.

 Immigration Status and Changes to Medi-Cal Eligibility (status table by population) DHCS
California Premium Subsidy $300 million in 2027, up to 200% FPL

A state program that supplements — and does not replace — the federal premium tax credit, preserving $0-premium Silver plans up to 150% FPL and lowering premiums from 150% to 200% FPL.

 Covered California Rates and Plans for 2027 (news release) Covered California
AB 2161's protective provisions Cover continued during the 30-day notice period, a data-conflict safeguard, and a hearing before any denial

New in 2026: (1) the county must continue Medi-Cal during the 30-calendar-day notice-of-noncompliance period for members who otherwise qualify; (2) when reliable data sources conflict adversely, the county must ask the applicant to confirm before taking adverse action; (3) the county must check every other basis of eligibility, and must give written notice and a state hearing opportunity, before denying.

 AB 2161 (Bonta) — Medi-Cal eligibility: work or community engagement. Chapter 209, Statutes of 2026 (amends WIC § 14005.69) California Legislative Information (leginfo.legislature.ca.gov)
Member outreach already underway Notices mailed from late August 2026; work-rule insert in every application packet October–December 2026

DHCS also runs a text-message campaign and points members to dhcs.ca.gov/workrules, and says it will send more information about local work, volunteer, job-training and school opportunities before the end of 2026.

 MEDIL I 26-25 — Six-Month Renewals and Work and Community Engagement Rules (outreach letter, incl. 'Dear Medi-Cal Member' notice dated 2026-08-31) DHCS, Medi-Cal Eligibility Division
The deferral clause DHCS may defer any change that lacks an appropriation

WIC § 14005.69(r): if a change creates a fiscal impact requiring an additional appropriation that has not been made, "the department may defer implementation of the change until that additional appropriation is made." If it does so, DHCS must notify the fiscal and policy committees and publish the notice on its website. This is the mechanism by which individual parts of the 2027 changes could be delayed.
