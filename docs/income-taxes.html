INC-05 Rental losses can offset other income — up to a limit Rental real estate losses are passive by default and generally cannot offset non-rental income. Exception: with "active participation" (e.g., you make management decisions such as approving tenants, rental terms, repair expenditures), up to $25,000 of loss can offset nonpassive income if MAGI ≤ $100,000; phased down 50% of the excess until fully phased out at MAGI $150,000. A separate "real estate professional" exception exists (Publication 925).
