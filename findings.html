Method and limitations

What was checked

  • The statute and CRS summary for the rule, effective date, activities, income test, exemptions, penalties and six-month renewals.
  • The CMS interim final rule, its June 29, 2026 correction, the CMS fact sheet, the December 2025 CIB, and the July 2026 IFR slide deck for the federal implementation details, including in-kind work and the MAGI income methodology.
  • DHCS ACWDL 25-30, MEDIL I26-25, the 2026 FPL letters and charts, the Medi-Cal Changes page, the Tracking page, and the H.R.1 implementation plan for California's dates, income test, exemptions and outreach.
  • Covered California's 2026 Open Enrollment financial details ($62,600) and 2027 FPL percentage ranges; the applicable FPL reference years differ from current-year Medi-Cal.
  • IRS publications and 42 CFR 435.603 for MAGI, rental, interest, capital gains and the no-asset-test rule.
  • IRS recordkeeping guidance supports keeping complete transactions. The form, if any, issued for these contracts is unknown and left blank.

What was not done

  • No personal eligibility determination. Age, household size, county, income amounts, disability/pregnancy status and current coverage were not supplied.
  • No tax return, account access, or Kalshi account data was used.
  • No legal advice on the Kalshi classification. The site flags the unresolved question and recommends a CPA.
  • No full end-to-end audit of the 135-page IFR; the relevant sections were checked against the Federal Register text and the CMS summary deck.
  • The older detailed pages and the three supplementary dashboards were preserved, not re-certified line by line by this session.
