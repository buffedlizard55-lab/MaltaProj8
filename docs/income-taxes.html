INC-04 Rental income (property management of own properties) Net rental income reported on Schedule E flows into AGI (Form 1040 line 8) and therefore into MAGI for Medi-Cal and Covered California purposes. Rental income is not "earned income" for Social Security purposes, but it is fully countable for coverage. Net Schedule E rent → AGI → MAGI Current law ✓ Verified IRS — Instructions for Schedule E (Form 1040) & Form 1040 instructions
Schedule E net profit/loss is reported on Form 1040 (rental real estate, royalties, partnerships, S corps). Verified via IRS Schedule E instructions (2025 ed.).
INC-05 Rental losses can offset other income — up to a limit Rental real estate losses are passive by default and generally cannot offset non-rental income. Exception: with "active participation" (e.g., you make management decisions such as approving tenants, rental terms, repair expenditures), up to $25,000 of loss can offset nonpassive income if MAGI ≤ $100,000; phased down 50% of the excess until fully phased out at MAGI $150,000. A separate "real estate professional" exception exists (Publication 925). This can legitimately lower AGI — and therefore MAGI for coverage. $25,000 special allowance; MAGI $100k–$150k phaseout Current law ✓ Verified IRS — Publication 527, Residential Rental Property
Verified against Pub 527 and 2025 Instructions for Form 8582 (irs.gov). Real-estate-professional test is complex — see flagged item FL-05.

INC-10 Trading gains are NOT self-employment income Gains/losses from selling capital assets (stocks, futures-type positions) held for investment are excluded from net earnings from self-employment (statute: 26 U.S.C. §1402(a)(3)(A); see Schedule SE instructions exclusion list) — unless you are a dealer in securities/commodities. Practical effect for you: no 15.3% SE tax on Kalshi results; but the gains still count for MAGI. Also means no SE-tax-based "work" signal — see the 2027 Medi-Cal work-requirement analysis. Excluded from SE tax (non-dealer) Current law ✓ Verified IRS — Instructions for Schedule SE (Form 1040) + Self-employment tax page
Statutory basis 26 U.S.C. §1402(a)(3)(A). See https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1040se for the "income not included" list.
