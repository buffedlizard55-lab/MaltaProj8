COVERAGE WITHOUT WAGES

Healthcare without earned income

A practical, evidence-linked guide for a U.S. citizen residing in California, with rental income, savings interest, and own-account trading.

THE SHORT ANSWER

$0 earned income does not automatically mean you fail.

The income route is not wages-only. Countable rental and interest income can matter for both the $580 community-engagement threshold and Medi-Cal’s separate upper income limit. Your actual eligibility is not established here.

Do not assume “no earned income” means “no income.” Rental profit, interest, and countable trading income can affect financial eligibility. Do not report $0 total income solely because you have no wages. Kalshi-specific classification and your personal figures remain unverified.
