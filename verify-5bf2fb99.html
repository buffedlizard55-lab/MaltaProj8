Your question — answered and re-verified

Profile: U.S. citizen, California permanent resident, property manager + full-time Kalshi trader, income only from Kalshi futures trading contracts, rental income, savings interest — 0 earned income (no W-2, no Schedule C wages unless elected).

Re-verified answer: YES — you can fulfill the work requirement with $0 earned income. The federal statute (P.L. 119-21 §71119) creates an income alternative: monthly income ≥ $580 (80 × $7.25 federal minimum wage). California DHCS guidance states this is “total gross income from any source” (ACWDL 25-30 p.7) and the member notice says “income from any source of at least $580 in any month since your last renewal” (MEDIL I26-25 p.2). Rental net, savings interest, and capital gains (including Kalshi gains under capital treatment) all count — summed in the month. You need only one qualifying month per 6-month window; no volunteering, no school, no 80 hours required if the income test is met. If all income is $0 for 6 consecutive months, 6 fallback paths exist.

Sources: H.R.1 §71119 on congress.gov · DHCS ACWDL 25-30 p.7 (Dec 30 2025) · DHCS MEDIL I26-25 p.2 (Sep 1 2026) · 91 FR 33348 (CMS-2454-IFC, Jun 3 2026)
