Executive Summary

How a California resident with $0 earned income obtains healthcare — and how "work requirements" actually apply to you.

1. The one idea that unlocks your situation

Health-coverage eligibility in California is not based on having a job. Medi-Cal and Covered California measure Modified Adjusted Gross Income (MAGI) — your federal tax return's AGI plus a few add-backs (healthcare.gov, INC-01/02). Kalshi trading gains, net rental income, and savings interest are all in that number; wages are irrelevant if they are zero. There is currently no work requirement to get Medi-Cal (until Jan 1, 2027) and no work requirement at all for Covered California financial help (MED-05, LAW-09). Your leverage is therefore tax-basis income management, not employment.

3. What the new federal law (P.L. 119-21, July 4, 2025) changes for you

  • Enhanced premium tax credits are gone. They expired Dec 31, 2025 and Congress did not renew them; 2026 Marketplace premiums jumped (Covered CA: applicants pay the old federal schedule — 2.10%–9.96% of income by band, nothing above 400%). California backfilled only the low end: ≤165% FPL in 2026, ≤200% FPL in 2027 (LAW-02/11, MKT-03–06).
  • APTC repayment is uncapped from the 2026 tax year. If you estimate income low and have a big winning trading year, you repay the full excess credit at tax time (LAW-03). Mitigation: apply honestly-close, update your estimate when your trading year turns, or take the credit as a lump sum at filing (MKT-10/12).

4. What California is doing for people with $0 earned income

  • California Premium Subsidy (Health Care Affordability Reserve Fund): $190M for 2026 (holds 0% benchmark contribution ≤150% FPL; extra help to 165%), $300M for 2027 (extends to 200% FPL, ~500k projected recipients) (LAW-11).
  • Medi-Cal with no asset test — the only program that ignores your savings entirely for 19–64 adults (MED-04); DHCS "no property limits."
  • Bronze/Minimum-Coverage plans made HSA-eligible effective 2026 — a California-specific ACA change that gives non-wage earners a legal MAGI-reduction tool (INC-16).
  • Individual-mandate safety valves: exempt if household income is below the federal filing threshold, if benchmark premiums exceed 7.28% of income (2025), or if the uninsured gap is ≤3 months (MND-04–06) — all realistic for $0-wage years.
  • County safety-net programs exist in some counties but were not verified for this report → ⚑ FL-08.

5. Bottom line — a practical playbook

  1. Project 2027 MAGI now (net trading + net rent + interest). If ≤ $22,025 (2026 basis; 2027 limits publish Jan 2027): apply for Medi-Cal today — no enrollment window, immediate start, no work test until 2027.
  2. If above that: apply Nov 1, 2026 – Jan 31, 2027 on Covered CA (one application screens Medi-Cal too). In the 138–200% band expect ~$0–5% of income for a Silver plan; pick Enhanced Silver (94/87) for near-zero deductibles (ACT-03, MKT-07).
  3. Protect yourself from the uncapped clawback: conservative estimate + prompt updates, or claim the credit entirely at filing (ACT-06).
  4. Document a 2027 work-requirement position now: keep the $580+/month income trail, and decide whether to add volunteer hours, a half-time course, or medical-frailty documentation (ACT-08, ⚑ FL-02/03).
  5. In high-gain years, run the tax levers that are actually verified: $3,000 capital-loss offset, up-to-$25,000 rental-loss special allowance if you qualify (⚑ FL-05), and HSA contributions on a Bronze plan — each reduces AGI, the number the programs re-test at reconciliation.
