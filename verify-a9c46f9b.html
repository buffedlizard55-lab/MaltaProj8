Verdict — do you need earned income?

No. For Medi-Cal: your existing rental net + Kalshi gains + savings interest in any one month since last renewal satisfies the $580 income test — no W-2, no 80 hours, no volunteering required if you are above $580 that month. If a month falls below $580, you may use the 6-month average, or fall back to 80 hours of volunteering / community service / half-time education / work program. For Covered California marketplace: there is no work requirement at all — income just sets subsidy size.
