Document only what actually happened

Income alternative

Save interest statements and rental records showing receipts, allowable expenses and any loss limitations. Keep detailed trade records; obtain advice on unresolved event-contract tax treatment. A deposit or withdrawal is not a pre-verified MAGI number. IRS rental rules · DHCS MAGI chart.

Activity / exception

Record actual dates/hours and ask the county what qualifies. Respond to any notice promptly. California law specifies verification, notice, response, continuing coverage during the statutory process and review of other bases; these are procedural rights, not guaranteed lifelong eligibility. §14005.69(k)–(r).

Do not use the old example table that treated bank deposits and hypothetical Kalshi gains as automatically countable or averaged every fluctuating month. It depended on unverified tax treatment and a broader averaging rule than the enacted statute. See the dated accuracy update and the unresolved-items register.
