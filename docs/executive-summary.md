# California healthcare with no earned income — evidence-based decision guide

**Updated 18 October 2026.** For a U.S. citizen who lives in California and reports rent, savings interest and Kalshi trading but no wages. This is a guide to the rules, **not a determination of this person's eligibility**: age, household size, actual countable income, Medicare status, health coverage, work-rule exemptions and the federal tax treatment of the trades have not been established. Do not use a gross-deposit total or assume a particular tax return schedule for Kalshi.

**Bottom line.** Earned income is **not** required to apply for MAGI Medi-Cal or Covered California. Starting **January 1, 2027**, some adult Medi-Cal applicants/members will have to satisfy the federal community-engagement rule or qualify for an exception. One route is monthly income at least **$580** (80 hours × the $7.25 federal minimum wage), measured using **MAGI-based financial methods for the person's MAGI-based household**. Rental and interest income can be countable, but their treatment and the treatment of Kalshi trades must be verified; the person's actual qualifying income is unknown. **Six-month averaging is specified for seasonal workers, not a blanket option for lumpy investment income.** See [California Welfare & Institutions Code §14005.69(e)(6)–(7)](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billNavClient.xhtml?bill_id=202520260AB2161), [42 CFR §435.552(f)–(g) in the CMS interim final rule](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/06/03/2026-11094/medicaid-program-community-engagement-requirement-for-certain-individuals) and [DHCS MAGI income chart](https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/MAGI-INCOME-AND-DEDUCTION-TYPES.pdf).

## Work/community-engagement rule: use the operative text, not a gross-receipts shortcut

[The federal rule, 42 CFR §435.552](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/06/03/2026-11094/medicaid-program-community-engagement-requirement-for-certain-individuals), implements [P.L. 119-21 §71119](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/1/text). [California's AB 2161, enacted §14005.69](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billNavClient.xhtml?bill_id=202520260AB2161), supplies state-specific procedures. DHCS dates work-rule implementation **January 1, 2027**, but dates **six-month eligibility checks to March 1, 2027**; a work check and a financial eligibility check are different things ([DHCS federal-impact timeline](https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/tracking-federal-impact-medi-cal-eligibility/)). Do not claim that every member has a six-month financial renewal on Jan 1.

The earlier [ACWDL 25-30](https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/25-30.pdf) uses “total gross income”; the later enacted statute and federal rule control the described methodology. | [AB 2161, §14005.69(e)(6)](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billNavClient.xhtml?bill_id=202520260AB2161) · [CMS interim final rule, §435.552(f)](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/06/03/2026-11094/medicaid-program-community-engagement-requirement-for-certain-individuals) |
