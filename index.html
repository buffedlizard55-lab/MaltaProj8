Executive Summary · P.L. 119-21 · H.R.1 §71119 · CMS-2454-IFC

You do not need earned income to keep healthcare in California.

The Medicaid “work requirement” is a community engagement requirement. One of 7 paths is enough, and the easiest — $580/month from any source — counts your existing net rental + Kalshi gains + bank interest. No W-2 needed. Your Answer in 30 Seconds

Profile: U.S. citizen, permanent California resident, property manager + full-time Kalshi trader, income only from Kalshi futures contracts, rental income, and savings interest — 0 earned income (no W-2, no Schedule C wages).

Can you fulfill the work requirement with 0 earned income? YES. You satisfy it with your existing unearned income via the $580/month income test. DHCS ACWDL 25-30 counts total gross income from any source (including rental net, interest, capital gains, unemployment, retirement, dividends). If rental ($800) + interest ($100) + any Kalshi gain ≥ $580 in any one month since last renewal, you pass — no volunteering, no school, no 80 hours needed. If all income is $0 for 6 straight months (rare with rental), you have 6 fallback paths (volunteer, education, work program, hardship, exemption).

Source: DHCS ACWDL 25-30, p.7 (Dec 30 2025) · DHCS MAGI Income Chart · 91 FR 33348 (CMS-2454-IFC)

What California Is Doing to Help Those with 0 Earned Income

✓ Passive income counts toward $580

DHCS counts net rental (Schedule E), interest (taxable + tax-exempt), capital gains, dividends toward work threshold. No “earned” restriction. DHCS MAGI chart →

✓ 6-month averaging for irregular income

Seasonal/lumpy Kalshi profits averaged over prior 6 months to meet $580. Protects traders with zero-income months. ACWDL 25-30 p.7 →

✓ Ex parte before asking you

Counties must check EDD/FTB/UI data first. You only respond if auto-verification fails — then 30 days to reply, coverage continues during cure. CMS IFC →

✓ All hardship exemptions adopted

CA applies disaster-county + high-unemployment-county exemptions automatically by residence; inpatient/SNF + medical travel by request. DHCS Plan →

✓ Lawsuit + outreach

Lead plaintiff in Massachusetts v. Oz (June 2026) challenging IFC + 3-month advance + periodic outreach per MEDIL I26-25. Tracking page →

✓ $1.1B to implement

Governor’s 2026-27 budget adds $1.1B General Fund to minimize procedural coverage loss. CalHHS webinar Feb 2026 →

Risks & What Could Go Wrong

Procedural loss is the biggest risk. 60–70% of projected national coverage losses are people who remain income-eligible but miss the 30-day cure window or mail. Watch your county mail at every 6-month renewal. Keep your address current with your county.
Kalshi classification risk: If your Kalshi trades are later treated as gambling, losses only offset winnings as itemized deductions (Schedule A) and are capped at 90% from 2026 — you pay tax on $10k phantom income even if break-even on $100k/$100k. Capital treatment lets losses offset gains on Schedule D. Confirm treatment with a CPA; do not rely on Kalshi’s 1099-INT (trading P&L is not on any 1099 — self-report required).
