The Zero-Earned-Income Playbook

You need one qualifying path in at least one month per 6-month renewal window. You do not need all seven, and you do not need earned income. Below is every path ranked for your facts, with what to file, what to document, and the California-specific wrinkle.

TL;DR for you: Path 1 (Income Test) is your standing compliance. Your existing rental net + savings interest + net Kalshi gains — summed to gross monthly ≥ $580 — satisfies the requirement with no hours, no volunteering, no school. California only checks one qualifying month since last renewal. Keep bank statements + Schedule E / 1099-INT + Kalshi P&L. Fallbacks are P3–P5 only if all countable income is $0 for 6 straight months.

Path 1 in Detail — What Counts Toward $580 for You

California’s ACWDL 25-30 says: “An individual’s total gross income for a month must meet or exceed [ $580 ]” and notes that “seasonal workers must average $580 monthly over the last six months.” The same guidance lists as countable sources: unearned buckets such as unemployment benefits, State Disability Insurance, retirement income, dividends. The DHCS MAGI Income Chart lists as countable: interest (taxable and non-taxable), rental real estate (Schedule E), capital gain (Schedule D) — all your streams.

California will tell you when to prove it. At each 6-month renewal, the county first checks its data. Only if it cannot find $580 in any month since last renewal (or 80 hrs, or exemption) will it send you a notice. You have 30 days to reply (coverage continues during cure). Per MEDIL I26-25 p.2: “Have an income from any source of at least $580 … in any month since your last renewal.”

How to Document Path 1 — Checklist

📄 Rental income

Keep: Lease(s), Schedule E, 1099-MISC (if PM reports rent), bank deposit records, expense receipts. Amount that counts: Net (gross minus deductible expenses) — but your bank deposits of net cash flow are what DHCS will see if verifying via bank statements/ex parte. Show at least one month ≥ $580 net.

📄 Savings interest

Keep: 1099-INT, monthly bank statements showing interest credits. Amount: gross interest (taxable + tax-exempt both count).

📄 Kalshi net gains

Keep: Kalshi P&L statement (annual, FIFO), your Form 8949/Schedule D workpapers with cost basis + proceeds per trade, bank withdrawals/deposits tying to trades. Kalshi does NOT issue a 1099 for trading P&L — you must self-report; don’t assume silence from Kalshi means $0.

📄 If challenged — income affidavit

Counties accept: pay stubs, employer verification, self-employment records (Schedule C/E, business bank statements), bank statements showing regular deposits, tax returns. File all three types together to “stack” to $580: e.g., one bank statement showing $300 rental deposit + $280 Kalshi withdrawal = $580.

Fallback Paths — If All Income Is Zero for 6+ Months

Volunteer 80 hrs/mo

Keep timesheets signed by supervisor; org name, dates, hours. California will email/mail a list of opportunities before renewals. 20 hrs/week. Source: MEDIL I26-25.

Enroll half-time

Enrollment verification letter from registrar; ~6 credits/term. Even career/technical training qualifies. Less-than-half-time: add work/vol hours to reach 80.

Work program 80 hrs/mo

EDD/CalJOBS workforce programs; entrepreneurship bootcamps if recognized. Ask county for approved-program list before enrolling.

Hardship exemption

Disaster county or high-unemployment county: no application, auto by residence. Inpatient/SNF: submit discharge paperwork. Medical travel: letter from provider. See California page →.

What NOT to do: Do not reduce countable income to stay “low” — there is no minimum-income disqualification for Medi-Cal; lower income helps eligibility, and the $580 test is a floor you want to clear. Do not report Kalshi as gambling losses on Schedule A to lower the $580 tally — gambling losses are itemized and do NOT reduce MAGI, so they hurt (not help) your subsidy/Medi-Cal MAGI while the phantom 90% cap creates tax you don’t owe under capital treatment.
