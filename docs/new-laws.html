The New Law — P.L. 119-21 (H.R.1, "One Big Beautiful Bill" / "Working Families Tax Cut") and What California Is Doing

Signed July 4, 2025. Three provisions drive everything in this report: (1) expiry of enhanced ACA premium tax credits after 2025, (2) uncapped APTC repayment from the 2026 tax year, and (3) new Medicaid work/community-engagement requirements effective January 1, 2027. California's response is targeted state subsidy money and a specific implementation posture.

LAW-02 Enhanced premium tax credits expired The ARPA (2021)/IRA (2022) enhanced PTCs — which raised credit amounts, set 0% contributions up to 150% FPL and removed the 400% FPL cap — expired December 31, 2025 and were not renewed. For 2026 federal law returns to the pre-2021 structure (credit ends at 400% FPL). Expired 12/31/2025 Jan 1, 2026 ✓ Verified IRS — Working Families Tax Cuts; Covered California — Important Changes

If your actual MAGI greatly exceeds your estimate, the full excess is repaid via Form 8962. LAW-04 Medicaid work requirement — what it is Section 71119 of H.R.1 adds a work/community-engagement condition for certain Medicaid adults: non-pregnant adults 19–64, not on Medicare, eligible under the ACA adult-expansion group. To enroll or renew they must meet ONE of: ≥80 hours in a month of work, community service or a work program (or any combination); enrollment at least half-time in an educational program; OR monthly income not less than 80 × the federal minimum wage ($580/month in 2026). 80 hrs/mo or $580/mo or half-time school Jan 1, 2027 (states may go earlier) ✓ Verified CMS — Medicaid Community Engagement Requirement IFR fact sheet (CMS-2454-IFC)
CMS Interim Final Rule issued June 1, 2026 with comment period. Applies to the 43–44 expansion states incl. California.
LAW-05 How compliance is tested — timing States verify at application and at renewal (optionally more often). New applicants must have met the requirement for at least the one month before their application month; current enrollees must show one or more qualifying months between renewals. If the state can't verify, a 30-day cure window follows a non-compliance notice; if disenrolled, the person may reapply at any time and is re-assessed. 1-month look-back; 30-day cure; reapply anytime From Jan 1, 2027 ✓ Verified CMS — IFR fact sheet ("Assessing Community Engagement")
LAW-06 Who is EXEMPT Exempt from the requirement: pregnant/postpartum; disabled or medically frail; parents/caretakers/family caregivers of a dependent child ≤13 or a person with a disability; former foster youth (under 26); American Indians/Alaska Natives; veterans with a total disability rating; people meeting TANF work rules or in SNAP-able households not exempt from SNAP work rules; people in drug/alcohol treatment; current inmates. States may also grant short-term hardship exceptions (inpatient care, county unemployment ≥8% or 1.5× national, disasters, travel for specialized care). 11 exemption categories + hardship options From Jan 1, 2027 ✓ Verified CMS — IFR fact sheet (Exemptions table)
